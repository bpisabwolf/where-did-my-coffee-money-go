\# Where Did My Coffee Money Go?



A web application that shows publicly disclosed corporate financial activity:

international investments, counterparties, lobbying, political contributions,

charitable grants, settlements, and other sourced money movements.



\## MVP



\- Search for one company

\- Display sourced financial activities

\- Show country and counterparty

\- Link every claim to its original source

\- Let users follow up to three companies

\- Send one weekly email digest
